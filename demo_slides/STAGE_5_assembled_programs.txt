====================================================================
  STAGE 5 — Assembled Programs
  Source: src/tritonflow/emit/assemble.py
====================================================================

After instruction selection, TritonFlow assembles the final Program:
a structured list of instructions in three sections:
  - PREAMBLE: one-time setup before the loop (constants, tile counts)
  - LOOP BODY: repeated once per K-tile iteration
  - EPILOGUE: post-loop operations (bias, relu, store)

The SAME kernel. THREE different chips. Only the YAML schema changes.
